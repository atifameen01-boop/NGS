SeqSim Studio Pro Educator Guide

                    
Using Next-Gen Sequencing Simulation in Bioinformatics Teaching

                

            

            
            

                
1. Reference Querying: Retrieve genomic regions directly from NCBI Entrez or Ensembl using live REST calls, or choose built-in gene panels (*TP53*, *HBB*, *EGFR*, *SARS-CoV-2 Spike*).

                
2. NGS Parameter Simulation: Teach students how read depth, sequencing error profiles (Illumina vs Nanopore), and quality score decay influence alignment and variant calling confidence.

                
3. Mutagenesis & VAF: Inject custom Single Nucleotide Variants (SNVs) or Insertions/Deletions with controlled Variant Allele Frequencies (e.g. 50% heterozygous germline or 15% subclonal somatic cancer mutation).

                
4. Interactive IGV Alignment: Explore how soft-clipping, coverage spikes, and mismatches appear in actual alignment browsers.

                
5. Manuscript Generator: Automatically synthesize simulated results into publication-ready manuscripts complete with LaTeX formulas ($Q = -10 \log_{10}(P)$), results tables, and literature references.

                
6. Downstream Compatibility: Export standard `.fastq`, `.sam`, and `.vcf` formats compatible with Galaxy, BWA, Bowtie2, Samtools, and GATK training workflows.
